# Wanliu Growth OS — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-31

Wanliu Growth OS is designed to run locally. Workspace records, drafts, experiments, leads, and analytics are kept on the operator’s machine unless the operator explicitly connects an external service.

When a social account is connected, the provider’s OAuth flow may return account identifiers, profile information, and access tokens needed for the requested integration. Tokens are kept in the local Postiz/Wanliu setup and are not intentionally sold or shared. Wanliu does not request or store social-account passwords.

The operator controls connected accounts and may revoke access through the provider or delete local integration data. Because deployments can be self-hosted, operators are responsible for securing their machine, backups, and logs. Wanliu does not knowingly collect data from children.
